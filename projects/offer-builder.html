Salon offer builder

Consulting work for a salon color sales rep. Walk a new salon through each product line and get promo order codes, one right-sized starter kit, and copy-paste lines for the ERP.

The problem

Opening a new salon account meant walking the owner through every color line, each with its own intro offer, then translating the picks into promo order codes for customer service. Stacking two lines could mean two sets of the same capes, bowls and color cards.

What I built

A single-page tool the rep uses with the salon owner. Each product line is one step with fixed quantity tiers. Each tier maps to one order code and lists the free goods it carries. Shared tools come from one starter kit, sized by total color units across all lines, so stacking brands never duplicates kit items. A meter shows how many more units unlock the next kit.

The second tab turns the order into numbered lines with codes, plus everything the salon gets free, in plain text ready to paste into the ERP. The third tab is the full mapping chart, so the rules are visible and easy to update.

How AI fit in

The offer rules came from the rep's intro offers. I built the page with Claude. Prices in the prototype were estimates worked back from those offers, with placeholders where the real mapping was still pending.

The brand, product names, prices and codes in this demo are invented.
