Segments that say what to do

The old lists sorted partners by revenue. That told you who mattered last year, not who to call this week. I replaced the bands with recency:

  • Active: sold in the last 90 days.
  • Slipping: 4 to 12 months quiet. The best call list.
  • Dormant: 13 to 24 months quiet.
  • Lapsed: 25+ months quiet. Archive.

The goal was to grow Active. Partners were also tiered by how much attention they get: Own It (named focus), Convert It (sequenced outreach and engineer touches), Automate It (newsletter and self-serve).

The +28.5% I didn't claim

A few weeks after outreach started, partners I had touched were up 28.5% on trailing 12. Untouched partners were down 11.1%. It would have been easy to put that on a slide.

It wasn't impact. Outreach had started weeks earlier, so trailing 12 mostly reflected buying from before I took over. The gap proved the targeting picked the right partners. It did not prove the outreach caused anything. I froze the touched and untouched baseline and set a date to rerun it once the window was fair.

Results

I finished at 166% of a $450K monthly quota. September was up 7.4% year over year, across 90 buying partners. I also ran overlap outreach to about 25 partners alongside a senior partnerships exec, a six-issue partner newsletter, a product launch campaign that reached about 1,030 people, and vetting for about a dozen partner applications.
