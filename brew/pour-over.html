Questions

What grind setting should I use for a V60?

It depends on the grinder and the coffee. On a Baratza Encore, Baratza's own starting point is 15. On a Comandante C40, most people start in the low to mid 20s in clicks. Light, dense coffees go finer. Dark roasts go coarser. The tool adjusts for both.

How do I know if my grind is right?

Watch the drawdown. If the water finishes well after the target time and the cup is bitter, go coarser. If it races through and tastes sour or thin, go finer.

Do I need a gooseneck kettle?

It helps you pour slowly and evenly, which matters most on a V60. You can make good coffee without one, but your drawdown times will bounce around more.

Why is the water so hot for light roasts?

Light roasts are dense and hold on to their sugars and acids. Water near boiling pulls them out. Dark roasts are the opposite and taste ashy with water that hot.
