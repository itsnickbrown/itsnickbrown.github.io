Questions

Can my grinder make espresso?

Some can't. The Fellow Ode, Timemore C2 and OXO Brew grinder don't have fine enough steps. The original Baratza Encore and Virtuoso+ can only work with a pressurized basket. The tool tells you when that's the case.

What ratio should I use?

1:2 is the usual start, so 18 grams in and 36 grams out. Light roasts often taste better longer, around 1:2.5. Dark roasts often taste better shorter, around 1:1.8.

My shot runs too fast. What do I change?

Grind finer, one step at a time. Keep the dose and yield the same so you know the grind made the difference.

Why does fresh coffee shoot so fast?

Beans under about a week off roast are full of gas. It pushes water through the puck unevenly. Let them rest to about day 7, or grind a step finer than usual.
