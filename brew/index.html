Questions

Why start from the coffee and not the machine?

Fellow's presets are built around roast level. Two light roasts can need different settings. A washed Ethiopian wants hot water and lots of agitation to show its florals. A natural from the same farm muddles if you push it that hard. Starting from the origin and process gets you closer on the first brew.

What if my grinder isn't listed?

Use the grinder closest to yours and treat the number as a direction. Most grinders work in the same order: lower is finer. If the cup is sour, go finer. If it's bitter, go coarser.

Single serve or batch?

Single serve uses the cone basket and pulses matter. Batch uses the flat bottom basket and one pulse. Batch beds drain faster, so the grind is usually a step coarser on the dial and you adjust from taste.

Why one change at a time?

If you change the grind and the temperature together and the cup gets better, you don't know which one did it. One change per brew gets you to a great cup faster.
