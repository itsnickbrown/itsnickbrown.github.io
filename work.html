How I'd learn your business.

No tool gets built until the work is understood. This is the order of operations.

1

Listen first

One-on-ones with the people doing the work, not just the people managing it. Sit in on the weekly reporting. Collect the spreadsheets people actually use, including the unofficial ones.

2

Map the work

List the recurring tasks, how long each takes and who touches the data. Find where two numbers that should match don't.

3

Pick two quick wins

One that saves time and one that fixes trust. Small enough to ship in weeks, visible enough that people notice.

4

Build it with the team

Build alongside the people who'll use it, write it down as a repeatable workflow, and train in the tools they already work in.

5

Measure, then repeat

Hours saved, adoption and error rates, before and after. Then the next item on the list.
